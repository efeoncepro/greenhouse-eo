# Evidence-led inbound recruiting and public vacancy writing

Load this reference before drafting or materially rewriting a public vacancy, job-board listing, vacancy campaign or employer-brand asset. Use it to make a candidate-facing offer more specific and more truthful; it does not authorize legal, payroll or compensation claims.

## The operating thesis

A public vacancy is a small conversion journey:

```text
discover → recognize the work → trust the conditions → show relevant evidence → apply → receive a clear next step
```

The internal brief is necessary but insufficient. It explains what Efeonce needs; the public offer must also explain why a qualified person should consider this work now, without inventing scope, benefits, scale or flexibility.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Greenhouse and approved business sources** — role owner, opening fields, compensation/benefit policy, employment model, Careers runtime and process. These decide what can be claimed.
2. **Primary research and authoritative guidance** — use for the copy method and selection safeguards; see Sources below.
3. **Current comparable postings** — use to understand market language and candidate expectations, never to copy claims or prose.
4. **Candidate voice** — use public portfolios, community discussions, interview notes or survey data only when lawful and relevant. Do not infer protected traits or use private social profiles as a screening mechanism.

When sources conflict, role truth and policy win. When a material fact is absent, show it as `needs confirmation`; do not write around the gap with vague language.

### Efeonce benefit-claim check

Before naming paid leave, read `docs/documentation/hr/sistema-permisos-leave.md` and verify the active policy for the intended engagement. The configured base currently distinguishes 15 paid vacation days, one paid floating holiday, paid bereavement, paid civic duty and paid medical/cita leave from study, personal, parental and unpaid leave, which are not paid by default. **Marriage leave is not a configured Greenhouse leave type.** These values and their applicability are policy/runtime facts, not reusable marketing copy; do not promise them to international contractor/EOR candidates until Payroll/Legal confirms their eligibility.

## Vacancy evidence packet

Before producing a final draft, record these facts in the job brief or working note:

| Field | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| Role truth | Why this role exists; manager; individual-contributor or manager; decision rights; real outputs and collaborators. |
| Success | Three to five observable 6–12 month outcomes, not a responsibility inventory. |
| Candidate evidence | Essential versus learnable skills; portfolio/work sample/interview evidence that will be assessed. |
| Working model | Employment or engagement path, country eligibility, working language, time-zone overlap, async/sync expectations and equipment/travel requirements. |
| Total offer | Approved compensation disclosure, benefits by engagement type, learning, flexibility and credible employer-brand proof. |
| Candidate journey | Application fields, stages, any exercise, accommodations, response commitment, owner and target timing. |
| Measurement | Source/UTM plan, funnel baseline and the experiment hypothesis. |

Do not publish a global/remote role until the working-model row is complete. A job may be fully remote while still having country, entity, payroll, overlap or legal constraints.

## Research protocol

### 1. Map the candidate market

For a material, senior, scarce or global role, benchmark at least five current comparable openings. Capture:

- searchable titles and skills;
- what the first fold promises;
- actual outputs and decision scope;
- location, work pattern, compensation and benefits disclosure;
- application evidence, process and CTA.

Synthesize a pattern, not a collage. Benchmarking should make Efeonce more precise, not make its voice sound like another company.

### 2. Select the one candidate promise

Choose one credible tension the role resolves. It must join a candidate ambition to an actual Efeonce mechanism. Examples of *shapes*, not reusable copy:

- make complex work clearer, not merely prettier;
- create craft that the next team can reuse rather than restart;
- own a defined product/campaign problem from evidence to delivery.

Reject promises such as “world-class”, “fast-paced”, “great culture”, “high impact” or “autonomy” unless the text immediately names the work, constraint and evidence behind them.

### 3. Convert internal facts into public proof

Use the mapping below:

| Internal fact | Candidate-facing expression |
| --- | --- |
| `role outcome` | What will be different or demonstrably better in 6–12 months. |
| `responsibility` | The real work and collaborators, grouped into a few readable bullets. |
| `competency` | A behaviour or portfolio/interview signal the person can evidence. |
| `benefit/policy` | Exact, eligible, bounded benefit; name the applicable engagement condition when needed. |
| `selection process` | Stages, what each assesses, timing and communication commitment. |

Never ask the candidate to decode internal vocabulary, scorecard IDs, seniority taxonomies or operating slogans.

### 4. Make the application low-friction and high-signal

- Ask for the smallest evidence set that lets Hiring screen fairly. For design, portfolio plus a short prompt about one real case is usually more diagnostic than a generic cover letter.
- Do not require applicants to restate a CV in a second form when the data is already available.
- Do not demand unpaid speculative work. If a practical exercise is necessary, make it short, job-related and paid; alternatively, allow an equivalent discussion of existing work.
- Publish the expected stages, likely timing, accommodation route and reply commitment. Candidate experience is part of the inbound offer, not an afterthought.

## Inclusion and fairness guardrails

- Write essential requirements as observable behaviours or outputs, not personality adjectives (“natural leader”, “hustler”, “rockstar”, “digital native”, “culture fit”).
- Keep essentials short and separate clearly from preferred or learnable capabilities.
- Do not require degrees, prestige employers, location or native-language labels unless legally and operationally indispensable.
- Do not use gender-coded, age-coded, ableist or identity-coded language. Do not use inferred personal data in attraction or selection.
- State how work will be assessed. The same job-related evidence must govern inbound, referral and outbound candidates.

## Senior creative individual-contributor roles

`Senior` is not shorthand for “manager”, “Art Director” or “strategy owner”. Before writing, determine whether the role is a hands-on IC, a craft lead, a people manager or an art-direction role; do not blur them.

For a Senior Visual/Brand/Graphic Designer, make scope tangible: campaign systems, identities, editorial/digital assets, presentation systems, production files, templates, critique or other artifacts that genuinely belong to the role. Explain the senior differentiator through judgment, end-to-end execution, production constraints, reusable systems and peer collaboration — not a decorative claim that the person “thinks strategically”.

Prefer portfolio evidence that asks: what was the problem, which visual decision mattered, what constraints shaped it and what changed. Do not treat “years of experience” or a named tool as a proxy for all of that.

## Remote and global vacancy contract

First fold must state the standardized role title, seniority, `100% remoto`, hiring region/country eligibility and CTA. The detail must answer:

- Where may the person legally be engaged, and by which model?
- What time-zone overlap, synchronous rituals and async documentation are expected?
- Which language(s) are operationally required and for what work?
- Which benefits apply to every eligible engagement, and which depend on contract, country or provider?
- What compensation band/currency or transparent disclosure path is approved?

Do not use “work from anywhere” if there are country restrictions, tax/visa restrictions, required overlap or different benefit terms. Google job markup requires a fully remote job to declare eligible applicant locations; the public copy must not contradict that runtime contract.

## Recommended public structure

1. Searchable title + modality, region and seniority.
2. First-fold candidate promise: the real problem, mandate and why the work matters.
3. Role mission and 3–5 observable success outcomes.
4. Real work: 4–6 grouped deliverables and collaborators.
5. Essential evidence, then optional/learnable capabilities.
6. What Efeonce supplies: exact benefits, learning and working model, with conditions.
7. Transparent process and assessment evidence.
8. Low-friction CTA plus a consent-governed Talent Pool alternative when the role is not a fit.

## Distribution and learning loop

Publishing is the start of inbound, not its completion.

- Keep one canonical Careers opening and detail/apply URL; every external channel links to it with source attribution.
- Match distribution to the evidence sought: portfolio communities and design networks for creative work; relevant regional and professional communities; referrals and consented Talent Pool for warm demand.
- Respect platform/group rules and never represent an approval queue as a live post.
- Do not turn every channel into the same announcement. Reframe the same verified role truth for the channel and link back to the canonical Careers URL. Never change eligibility, scope, compensation or selection criteria by channel.

## Full-funnel inbound operating model

`Inbound recruiting` is an operating label, not one scientifically isolated intervention. Treat it as the coordinated use of attraction, credible signalling, candidate experience, consented relationship management and learning. Research supports its components; do not make a blanket causal claim for the label itself.

| Stage | Candidate question | Required mechanism | Useful asset | Do not optimize for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | "Does this role exist and is it relevant to me?" | Searchable title, canonical page, channel fit and accessible distribution. | Careers page, role-specific social post, community contribution. | Impressions alone. |
| Recognize | "Is this really my kind of work?" | Specific work, context, autonomy, constraints and 6–12 month outcomes. | Vacancy first fold, case study, team/work explainer. | Generic employer-brand sentiment. |
| Trust | "Can I believe the offer and operate under these conditions?" | Bounded claims, realistic preview, compensation/location/process transparency. | Working-model FAQ, benefit policy, process timeline. | Aspirational copy that lacks proof. |
| Convert | "Can I show evidence without wasting time?" | Short, accessible, evidence-first application. | Portfolio/CV upload + 2–3 job-related prompts. | Raw application volume. |
| Assess | "Will I be evaluated fairly and respectfully?" | Structured, job-related process and timely updates. | Published stages, rubric-facing competency labels, accommodation path. | Interviewer intuition. |
| Nurture | "May I remain in touch for a future relevant role?" | Separate, revocable consent and useful future contact. | Talent Pool opt-in, relevant content/event invitation. | A database of unconsented contacts. |
| Learn | "Did this source and message produce a viable hire?" | Source-aware funnel and controlled experiments. | UTM taxonomy, dashboard, post-hire feedback loop. | Click-through rate in isolation. |

### Candidate-value and proof system

For each role, build a small message map before making content:

| Candidate value | Efeonce proof required | Public expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaningful craft or problem | Actual artifacts, decisions and collaborators in the mandate. | The work the person will make and the constraint it solves. |
| Growth or learning | Named practice, certification, coaching, training or real exposure; eligibility confirmed. | What the person can learn and through which mechanism. |
| Flexibility | Approved working pattern, time-zone/meeting expectations and engagement constraints. | Exact remote/hybrid model, not "flexible" by itself. |
| Fair reward | Approved range or an approved transparent disclosure path. | Range/currency and the criteria that affect it, where approved. |
| Belonging and respect | Observable inclusive practices, process design and actual team behaviour. | How decisions, feedback and accommodations work; not a culture slogan. |

If a claim has no proof, remove it or put it in the claim ledger as `needs confirmation`. Employee stories, client cases and social content are evidence only when they show the role-relevant mechanism and do not disclose confidential work or identify protected traits.

### Vacancy writing sequence

Use this order unless verified candidate research supports another one:

1. **Title, seniority, modality, eligible region and CTA.** Use a standard searchable title; do not add company, salary, hype or dates to structured-data job titles.
2. **Problem and promise.** State the real tension, mandate and why it matters to the person doing the work.
3. **Outcomes before inventory.** Name 3–5 observable results, then group the work that leads to them.
4. **Evidence required.** Present a short list of essential, job-related evidence, then clear learnable/preferred capabilities.
5. **What the organization supplies.** Give exact, bounded compensation, benefits, learning and operating-model information.
6. **Selection and next step.** Explain stages, evidence, likely timing, accommodation route and a short direct application.

The method intentionally gives needs–supplies information prominence without hiding demands–abilities. A qualified candidate needs both a reason to pursue the work and a truthful way to self-select.

## Candidate experience is part of acquisition

Treat every interaction after the click as employer-brand evidence.

- Provide a confirmation with the expected next step; do not promise a date the team cannot meet.
- Publish what each stage tests in candidate language. Explain why any exercise exists and how long it should take.
- Prefer a portfolio/previous work discussion or a paid, short, job-related exercise. Do not make a candidate solve a live client problem for free.
- Use structured interviews and consistent job-related criteria for inbound, referral and sourced candidates.
- Give timely, respectful closure. A rejection can protect reapplication and recommendation intent; silence cannot.
- Do not screen personal social media or infer identity, health, emotion, personality or protected characteristics. Public professional work is not a license for unstructured private-data collection.

The external process must match internal practice. If a process is not yet defined, fix the process before publishing it rather than inventing reassuring copy.

## Talent Pool and permission-based nurturing

An application to one opening is not permission to market future openings. Separate the two choices.

1. Offer an optional, unticked Future Opportunities/Talent Pool choice with a plain-language purpose, contact type and privacy link.
2. Record the source, purpose, timestamp, version of notice and withdrawal state. Use a confirmation flow where the runtime supports it.
3. Send only role-relevant contact at a credible cadence. Value can include relevant openings, useful craft content or an optional event; it is never an excuse for batch spam.
4. Honor opt-out/withdrawal immediately and maintain the minimal suppression control needed to avoid contacting the person again.
5. Set retention/review periods with Legal/Privacy. Do not retain portfolios, CVs or profile data indefinitely because they may be useful someday.

For consent, cross-border transfers, retention, legal basis and electronic communications, hand off to Legal/Privacy. This skill supplies the product and candidate-experience requirements, not a jurisdictional opinion.

## Compensation and conditions as trust signals

Compensation is not a cosmetic footer. It changes the candidate's search and self-selection behaviour.

- Publish an approved range and currency where policy/law permits or requires it. A range must be credible for the role and engagement, not nominally compliant.
- When a range is wide, state the legitimate role-related factors that determine the starting point and, where approved, the typical starting point. Do not suggest a false certainty.
- If a range is not approved, describe the disclosure milestone honestly; do not write "competitive" as a substitute for information.
- Separate universal benefits from benefits that vary by employee, contractor, EOR, country or provider. Never imply equivalence where terms differ.

## Measurement, diagnostics and experimentation

Create a source/UTM taxonomy before distribution. Measure the following cohort funnel by source, region and role family where volume makes interpretation safe:

```text
qualified visits → apply starts → completed applies → qualified screens
→ interview/assessment → offer → acceptance → 90/180-day outcome
```

Track, at minimum:

- canonical-page visits and apply-start rate;
- completion rate and form abandonment step;
- qualified-screen rate and advance rate by source;
- time to acknowledgement, stage-to-stage SLA and final closure;
- candidate-experience/fairness signal, where collected safely;
- offer acceptance, early retention and hiring-manager outcome by source;
- Talent Pool consent, reactivation and conversion separately from opening applications.

Do not rank channels from application volume alone. A source that produces fewer applications but stronger qualified screens, accepted offers and early outcomes may be more valuable. Do not compare sources until they have enough volume and comparable role/market conditions.

Run one controlled learning loop at a time:

1. State a falsifiable hypothesis, baseline, primary quality metric and guardrail metric.
2. Change one variable: first-fold proposition, proof order, CTA, application burden, channel or title/search term.
3. Preserve role truth, pay, eligibility and selection criteria across variants.
4. Evaluate qualified progression and candidate experience, not clicks alone.
5. Record the result, context and decision; do not generalize a result from one role or market automatically.

Examples: test a work-problem opening against a company-history opening; test portfolio-plus-one-case prompt against a generic cover letter; test a design community distribution against a broad listing. Never A/B test legal eligibility, accommodation availability, core requirements or an unapproved benefit claim.

## Evidence strength and limits

Use the strongest available source for each decision:

| Decision | Strongest available evidence | Practical conclusion | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Needs–supplies copy | Field experiment with 56 ads / 991 applicants. | Lead with a real candidate value proposition while keeping clear essentials. | One context; test against Efeonce roles. |
| Overall attraction | Meta-analysis of 71 studies / 667 coefficients. | Job/organization facts, fit, process and recruiter behaviour all matter. | Associations do not make a universal template. |
| Realistic preview | Meta-analysis of 40 studies. | Reveal relevant constraints early to improve self-selection and post-hire expectations. | Preview content must fit the actual work. |
| Candidate reactions | Meta-analysis of 86 samples / 48,750 participants. | Use face-valid, job-related, structured selection and transparent process. | Candidate favourability is not the sole validity criterion. |
| Gender-coded language | Experimental/archival research. | Remove stereotyped language and specify observable evidence. | A text scanner is a review aid, not a fairness guarantee. |
| Pay disclosure | Experiments and field research. | Publish credible pay information and explain ranges; expect sorting, not necessarily more volume. | Legal requirements and markets vary. |
| Platform/SEO | Official Google/Indeed documentation. | Use structured data and direct application consistently with visible job truth. | Discovery rules and platform effects can change. |
| Community/nurture | Recruitment-marketing literature and privacy rules. | Offer useful, consented relationship-building; measure later conversion. | "Inbound recruiting" as a label has less causal evidence than its components. |
