# Experience LaunchOps — Roadmap V1

> **Status:** Discovery / proposed. This is sequencing, not authorization for production implementation.
> **Epic:** [`EPIC-036`](../epics/to-do/EPIC-036-efeonce-experience-launchops.md)

## North star

Convert a validated enterprise launch bottleneck into a repeatable Wave product service with measurable reduction
of time-to-market, governed agentic delivery, and SEO/AEO/measurement by design.

## Sequence

| Wave | Focus | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Understand | Interviews, process map, ICP, buying group, baseline | Qualified pilot + quantified bottleneck |
| 1. Govern | Launch Policy Pack, risk classification, controls, RACI, exceptions and evidence model | Pilot governance pack and SOW skeleton |
| 2. Assure | Stack, threat model, cloud, SRE, legal/data, agent assurance and procurement pack | Enterprise readiness baseline |
| 3. Package | Offer, roles, scope, pricing and delivery model | Approved validation offer |
| 4. Specify | Launch Contract, Experience Spec, Search Contract, Measurement Contract, Brand/UI/UX artifact chain and quality rubric | One launch expressed as governed contracts |
| 5. Assist | Agent Fabric, core Workers, provider gateway, first custom Worker and optional Globe Creative Production Contract | Human review with provenance, asset/assembly manifests and measurable assisted work |
| 6. Launch | Thin control plane, CMS adapter, preview, approval, release, verification | One low-risk production launch with rollback reference |
| 7. Operate | Managed LaunchOps lane, lead-time instrumentation and reporting | Repeat launches with baseline/after improvement |
| 8. Scale | More adapters, sites, markets and Wave composition | Commercial approval based on margin, reliability and renewal |

## Guardrails

- Start as managed/productized service; do not assume SaaS or self-service economics.
- Lead with no rip-and-replace: observe/connect first, then add governed writes only where authorized.
- Choose the first CMS from pilot evidence; Modyo, Drupal/Acquia and headless are candidates, not commitments.
- Preserve human approval for legal, brand, privacy, security and production release.
- Treat Search Visibility 360 and Measurement & Analytics as composable lanes, not duplicated functionality.
- Keep Globe as a capability of platform + creative people + operating capacity; it may deliver Studio Access,
  Creative Production, Managed Squad or Staff Augmentation. Keep Efeonce Digital/Kortex as CRM/RevOps owner.
- Keep Globe composable: it may produce asset-ready or experience-ready packages, but Wave owns launch-ready
  assembly, Search/Measurement, governance and release.
- Every stage must produce evidence reusable for pricing, delivery, architecture and sales enablement.
- Every adapter must document the client system of record, integration mode, scopes, rollback and exit path.
- Measure operator leverage and specialist craft quality; headcount reduction is not a success metric.

## Roadmap decision gates

1. **Problem gate:** enough qualified demand and measurable cost of delay.
2. **Offer gate:** buyer, scope, delivery model and willingness to pay are coherent.
3. **Technical gate:** target stack supports preview, permissions, approvals, release and recovery.
4. **Value gate:** lead time/rework/quality improve without governance regression.
5. **Scale gate:** cost-to-serve, margin, capacity and renewal justify expansion.
