# Experience LaunchOps — Enterprise Readiness & Procurement Pack V1

> **Status:** Proposed / pre-sales enablement
> **Date:** 2026-07-26
> **Owner:** Commercial + Wave + Security/Privacy + Architecture + Legal/Finance

## 1. Purpose

Make Experience LaunchOps buyable by mid-market and enterprise procurement without overclaiming certifications,
security posture, operational maturity or legal compliance that has not been evidenced.

The primary enterprise adoption promise is **no rip-and-replace**: the service connects to the client's existing
platforms and preserves their system ownership, identity, approvals and investment. A migration is never a hidden
dependency of the product service.

## 2. Pack contents

- one-page product/service description and boundaries;
- identity/access architecture with OIDC/SAML/SCIM, tenant roles and deprovisioning behavior;
- reference architecture and data-flow diagram;
- governance/compliance operating model;
- security questionnaire response library;
- subprocessor/provider inventory and change notice process;
- MSA/SOW/DPA/NDA decision checklist;
- RACI and support/SRE model;
- implementation and onboarding plan;
- evidence-pack sample with synthetic data;
- risk register, exception process and incident contacts;
- accessibility, privacy, AI-use and data-retention statements;
- pricing/packaging assumptions and pass-through policy;
- integration posture matrix showing `observe-only`, `assistive`, `governed-write` and `client-operated` modes;
- insurance/corporate information handled by the appropriate Efeonce owner.

## 3. Evidence levels

Every statement is tagged `designed`, `implemented`, `tested`, `operationally verified` or `contractual`. Never use
“enterprise-grade”, “secure”, “compliant”, “certified” or “production-ready” without a defined claim and evidence.

## 4. Procurement objections to answer

- Where does data live and who can access it?
- Which providers/models/subprocessors are used?
- Can the client approve, export, revoke and delete?
- What happens when CMS, model, cloud or analytics fails?
- How are agents constrained and evaluated?
- Can the service coexist with the client’s CMS and approval process?
- What remains the source of truth in each platform, and what exactly does LaunchOps store or mutate?
- What is Wave versus Efeonce versus Globe versus Efeonce Digital?
- What is included in managed service, squad and staff augmentation?
- How are costs, usage, changes and incidents controlled?

## 5. Readiness gate

The pack is sales-ready only when each claim has owner, source, verification date, contract treatment and known
exception. A questionnaire answer cannot be improvised per opportunity; it must reconcile with the actual runtime,
policy and SOW.
