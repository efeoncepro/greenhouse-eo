# Experience LaunchOps — Legal, Contract & Data Processing Pack V1

> **Status:** Proposed framework — not legal advice or contract language for signature.
> **Date:** 2026-07-26
> **Owner:** Legal/Privacy/IP + Commercial + Wave

## 1. Contract stack

- NDA where discovery requires confidential information.
- MSA for relationship, roles, fees, confidentiality, liability, termination and dispute terms.
- SOW for launch scope, markets, CMS, environments, deliverables, approvals, RACI, service mode and evidence.
- DPA/data processing terms where Efeonce processes personal data for the client.
- Provider/subprocessor schedule for cloud, models, CMS, analytics and support tools.
- Change order and exception/risk acceptance records.

## 2. Required decisions before signature

Identify governing law/forum, controller/processor roles, data categories, jurisdictions, transfers/residency,
retention/deletion, security measures, breach notification, audit rights, subprocessors, client instructions,
production access, business continuity, suspension, indemnities, warranties, liability caps and insurance.

## 3. IP and content

Separate client data, client marks/claims, Efeonce/Wave methods and reusable platform IP, third-party licenses,
model/provider rights, stock/fonts/media, source code, templates, prompts, evaluation sets and portfolio rights.
Credits, fees or agent usage do not automatically transfer copyright, likeness, voice, exclusivity or third-party
licenses. Rights must be explicit and jurisdiction-aware.

## 4. Claims and outcome boundaries

Do not guarantee rankings, indexing, model citation, conversion, revenue, legal compliance or uninterrupted provider
availability unless separately defined, evidenced and contractually accepted. Define “published”, “verified”,
“measured”, “indexable” and “agent-ready” operationally.

## 5. Legal gate

No regulated pilot handles real personal/sensitive data, publishes regulated claims or activates a new subprocessor
until client Legal/Privacy/Security authority and qualified counsel approve the applicable contract and policy pack.

This is a framework; validate current law and final language with counsel in every applicable jurisdiction.
