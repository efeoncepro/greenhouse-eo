# Efeonce Candidate Benefits Charter

Use this reference when drafting the benefits section of any Efeonce vacancy, Careers page, job-board post or recruiting campaign. It is the approved candidate-facing baseline supplied by the operator on 2026-08-17. It is not a replacement for a signed agreement, local law, provider terms or the current Greenhouse Leave configuration.

## Claim status and publication rule

- The baseline below applies to **all Efeonce collaborators worldwide** as an Efeonce people policy.
- A vacancy may state the baseline once its country eligibility and engagement path are explicit. Do not imply that a global role is legally/employment-operationally ready merely because the benefits are global.
- Greenhouse does not yet model every item below. Never say a benefit is self-service, auto-approved, balance-tracked or available in the Leave portal unless the runtime proves it.
- Before an offer, Payroll/Legal must express the applicable baseline in the employee agreement, contractor agreement, EOR/provider instruction or equivalent governing document. Local statutory entitlements and mandatory provider rules may add protection; do not promise a reduction or waiver in public copy.
- Preserve medical privacy. Managers receive the operational absence needed to coordinate work, never a diagnosis or medical history.

## Global operating rules

These rules make the baseline predictable across countries and engagement models. The applicable local law or provider rule may improve them, never reduce them.

- **Benefit year and proration.** The benefit year is 1 January to 31 December. Vacation, floating days, wellbeing days, learning days and annual wallets are prorated by complete calendar months in the first and final year. A tenure vacation day is added on the first day after each completed continuous-service anniversary; it is not prorated.
- **Vacation carryover.** Carry up to 5 unused vacation days into the following year, to be used by 30 June. No statutory entitlement is forfeited where local law requires a longer carryover or a different treatment.
- **Continuous service.** Efeonce-approved leave and a documented transfer between Efeonce, its EOR or payroll provider do not break service. A voluntary or involuntary separation longer than 30 calendar days resets the Efeonce tenure clock unless local law requires otherwise.
- **Family definitions.** Immediate family means spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling, dependent household member or an equivalent culturally recognised close family relationship. Extended family means grandparent, grandchild, parent/sibling-in-law or equivalent close kin. People may resolve an ambiguity in favour of care responsibility, not formal paperwork.
- **No benefit penalty.** Approved leave, medical appointments, wellbeing days, maternity/paternity/adoption leave and lawful civic duty cannot lower performance ratings, promotion eligibility, variable compensation or access to learning.
- **Return after birth.** A returning birth parent receives four weeks of workload ramp and flexible scheduling at full contracted compensation. Nursing/lactation breaks and pregnancy-related accommodations follow the more favourable applicable law; managers plan coverage without demanding medical detail.
- **Contractor equivalence.** Employees and EOR workers receive these benefits through the applicable leave/provider mechanism. Contractors receive an equivalent paid non-availability entitlement in their service agreement, without falsely representing it as employee statutory leave or creating manager-controlled medical disclosure.
- **Coverage.** Planned leave is coordinated for coverage, not subject to arbitrary denial. A manager who cannot support the requested dates must propose a practical alternative promptly; urgent, statutory and health-related absence follows its own rule.

## Benefit wallets and financial controls

- Wallet caps are denominated in US dollars and converted into the agreement currency using Finance's published yearly rate. Annual wallets reset with the benefit year; the equipment cap follows its 36-month replacement cycle. They are reimbursements or approved direct purchases, not salary, cash substitution, bonus, carryover or a termination payment.
- Learning use requires a role-relevant development plan; equipment remains Efeonce property where local law and the agreement permit; connectivity/coworking and mental-health reimbursements require only the minimum receipt/provider evidence needed for Finance, never a diagnosis.
- Finance maintains a benefits cost centre and forecasts these wallets, paid leave and parental coverage quarterly from the active roster. People reports aggregate use and access by country/engagement; no individual health use enters performance reporting.
- Any change to the caps or a new wallet needs Finance approval, a written effective date and a contract/provider review before it is announced.

## Health, wellbeing and privacy guardrails

- up to 16 paid hours per calendar year, normally in blocks of up to 4 hours per planned appointment;
- give notice where reasonably possible and provide only a certificate of attendance, never a diagnosis;
- urgent care receives the time reasonably necessary without prior notice, then is regularized with the minimum attendance evidence;
- a formal medical licence and statutory preventive screenings are separate and do not consume this balance;
- People may approve an exception for recurring treatment, disability, pregnancy or care circumstances; a manager never decides from medical detail;
- once the balance is exhausted, People and the collaborator agree the lawful/contractual path (schedule flexibility, vacation, floating day or unpaid leave) without obstructing urgent or statutory care;
- do not use medical appointment use as performance evidence.

Wellbeing days require no medical evidence. Across remote work, use coverage plans and defined collaboration overlap rather than surveillance, out-of-hours response expectations or pressure to work while on leave.

## Learning and internationally recognised partners

Efeonce offers role-relevant learning and certification opportunities through internationally recognised partners, including HubSpot, Google, Meta, Salesforce, OpenAI, Automattic, Truora and Aircall, among others.

Use this as an opportunity statement, not a guarantee that every person receives every certification. Before naming a partner in a particular vacancy, confirm that the relationship, programme availability and marketing/logo rights remain current. Do not call someone “certified” unless the credential is actually earned and evidenced.

## Sources and interpretation boundaries

- Operator decisions in the 2026-08-17 benefits working session are the authority for this candidate-facing baseline, including its global-first design and 15-to-20 tenure progression.
- `docs/documentation/hr/sistema-permisos-leave.md` and `scripts/setup-postgres-hr-leave.sql` describe the currently implemented Leave state, which is narrower than this charter.
- This is a global policy, not a Chile policy with exceptions. Country law and EOR/provider terms are local overlays. Remote-company benchmarks inform the design, not entitlement calculation: [Remote](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/remotecom/jobs/7564657003?gh_src=b6d3dc383us), [Automattic](https://automattic.com/benefits/) and [Doist](https://doist.com/careers).
- The birth-parent entitlement recognises pregnancy and childbirth recovery separately from parental bonding. The ILO's 18-week recommendation is a global benchmark, not Efeonce's current financial commitment; a more favourable lawful entitlement always prevails: [ILO Recommendation No. 191](https://www.ilo.org/resource/other/r191-raccomandazione-sulla-protezione-della-maternit%C3%A0-2000) and [Care at Work](https://www.ilo.org/wcmsp5/groups/public/---dgreports/---dcomm/documents/publication/wcms_838653.pdf).
- This reference establishes candidate communication and an internal policy direction. It is not legal advice and does not by itself modify contracts, provider arrangements, payroll or statutory rights.
