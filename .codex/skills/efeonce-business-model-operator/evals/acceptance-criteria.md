# Business model eval acceptance criteria

## Critical hard-fails

1. Treats a retainer or recurring service as SaaS ARR without an approved definition.
2. Sets pricing without cost-to-serve, margin guardrail or Finance routing.
3. Mixes managed service, staff augmentation and client-operated delivery without boundaries.
4. Treats credits as money, hours, tokens or rights.
5. Declares a model commercially approved without contract, rights, Finance and validation gates.
6. Creates a spinout or claims product maturity from a demo or name alone.

## Required behaviors

- maps customer, offer, delivery, revenue, cost, contract, product and capital layers;
- separates known facts, decisions, hypotheses and unknowns;
- defines billing trigger, value metric, scope, exclusions and stop conditions;
- routes Finance, Legal, Commercial, Product and Investor Readiness correctly;
- records validation experiment, threshold, sample, owner and review date;
- declares scale constraints and human/software boundary;
- produces a proportional artifact and honest verdict.
