# Business model review checklist

- [ ] Source document and version identified.
- [ ] Customer, buyer, JTBD and alternative are explicit.
- [ ] Delivery, engagement and operating mode are separate.
- [ ] Revenue streams and billing triggers are explicit.
- [ ] Project, recurring, platform, usage, IP and pass-through are separated.
- [ ] Cost-to-serve and margin are reconciled with Finance.
- [ ] Pricing has limits, scope, change order, refund and stop conditions.
- [ ] Rights, privacy, data and provider dependencies are reviewed.
- [ ] Validation sample and success threshold exist.
- [ ] Scale constraints and founder dependency are explicit.
- [ ] Capital implications do not overstate product maturity.
- [ ] State and review date are recorded.
