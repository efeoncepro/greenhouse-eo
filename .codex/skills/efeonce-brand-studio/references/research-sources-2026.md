# Research sources — corte 2026-07-26

Usar estas fuentes como contexto fechado, no como verdad permanente. Revalidar tendencias volátiles antes de convertirlas en recomendación.

## Señales estratégicas

- [AMA Future Trends 2026](https://www.ama.org/marketing-news/2026-trends-report/): agentes autónomos, confianza, creatividad humana y storytelling.
- [Kantar Marketing Trends 2026](https://www.kantar.com/north-america/company-news/kantars-2026-marketing-trends): innovación brand-led, inclusión auténtica, plataformas coherentes y microcomunidades.
- [Kantar/MASB MDS](https://www.kantar.com/inspiration/brands/masb-certifies-kantars-meaningful-different-and-salient-framework): meaningful, different, salient y conexión con crecimiento.
- [Ehrenberg-Bass Distinctive Assets](https://marketingscience.info/learn-with-us/commercial-research/distinctive-asset): medir asociación única, no gusto interno.
- [Edelman Trust Barometer 2026](https://www.edelman.com/trust/2026/trust-barometer/special-report-brands): trust + relevance + earned evidence.
- [Gartner Trust Scarcity](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-12-in-the-ai-era-trust-scarcity-is-rewriting-the-rules-of-brand-growth): credibilidad, advocacy y expert proof como diferenciadores.

## IA, entities y discoverability

- [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): AI Overviews/AI Mode y query fan-out; no hacks especiales.
- [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): SEO y contenido útil como base.
- [Google Organization structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization): desambiguación de organizaciones y `sameAs`.
- [Google AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports): impresiones en superficies generativas.
- [Bing AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview): citaciones y grounding queries.
- [Webflow AI Discovery Gap](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/16/3328420/0/en/Webflow-Research-Finds-Most-Companies-Are-Unprepared-for-the-AI-Discovery-Era.html): señal de proveedor, no benchmark universal.
- [Generative Engine Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08919): preprint; tratar como evidencia emergente.
- [Synthetic sameness study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12827715/): evidencia experimental reciente, alcance aún limitado.

## Productización y governance

- [Frontify brand governance](https://www.frontify.com/en/guide/brand-governance-framework): roles, champions, formación y feedback.
- [Adobe brand consistency](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/perspectives/brand-consistency-at-scale): governance desde brief hasta publicación.
- [ManyRequests productized services](https://www.manyrequests.com/blog/productized-service-guide?1c88e415_page=2): alcance, intake, precio fijo y retainers.
- [Valore](https://valore.agency/): ejemplo de sprint inicial + governance retainer.

Registrar siempre fuente, fecha, mercado, método, nivel de confianza y si la conclusión es `evidence` o `inference`.
