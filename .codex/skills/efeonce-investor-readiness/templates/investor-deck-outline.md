> Base deck: 12–15 slides. Every slide must have an assertion, evidence, source, owner and confidence.

## Red-team

- [ ] Deck is understandable without a narrator.
- [ ] No slide relies on an unverified metric.
- [ ] Services, platform and software are not conflated.
- [ ] The ask is tied to milestones, not generic growth.
- [ ] Every objection has an answer or is explicitly open.
