## Transaction blockers

- [ ] cap table or ownership uncertainty;
- [ ] material revenue/customer concentration unexplained;
- [ ] IP ownership not documented;
- [ ] customer data or confidentiality issue;
- [ ] material tax/debt/related-party exception;
- [ ] instrument/jurisdiction unresolved;
- [ ] governance or exclusivity disproportionate.
