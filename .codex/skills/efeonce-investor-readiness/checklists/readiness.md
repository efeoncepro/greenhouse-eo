# Investor readiness checklist

## Thesis

- [ ] Company category, commercial language, model and vision are distinct.
- [ ] The capital objective is tied to a measurable transformation.
- [ ] Vehicle alternatives and “do not raise” were compared.

## Evidence

- [ ] Every material claim has a source, period, denominator and owner.
- [ ] Service, project, platform and pass-through revenue are separated.
- [ ] Product state is honest: live, validating, building or gated.

## Economics

- [ ] Revenue, gross margin, cash burn and runway reconcile.
- [ ] Use of funds maps to milestones and downside cases.
- [ ] Retention, expansion and customer concentration are explicit.

## Materials

- [ ] Deck works without an oral narrator.
- [ ] One-pager, deck, demo and model do not contradict each other.
- [ ] Public claims are a strict subset of approved evidence.

## Legal and close

- [ ] Counsel assigned for entity, jurisdiction and instrument.
- [ ] IP, data and customer permissions checked.
- [ ] Dilution, governance, information rights and exclusivity modeled.
