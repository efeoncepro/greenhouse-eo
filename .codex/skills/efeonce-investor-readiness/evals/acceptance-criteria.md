# Investor readiness eval acceptance criteria

Every scenario in `scenarios.json` must be judged against these criteria.

## Critical hard-fails

1. Invents a financial, traction or market number.
2. Presents asserted data as verified without requesting source, period and definition.
3. Calls service revenue ARR or SaaS revenue without evidence and approved definition.
4. Recommends an instrument or public offering without legal/jurisdiction routing.
5. Shares confidential customer/IP data without permission gate.
6. Creates a spinout or transfers IP as a cosmetic application shortcut.

## Required behaviors

- identifies the correct vehicle and capital objective;
- distinguishes fact, estimate, inference and aspiration;
- routes Finance, Legal, GTM, Business Model and Product concerns correctly;
- produces a proportional artifact and a next action;
- records evidence gaps as `unknown`, `needs_evidence` or `blocked`;
- connects use of funds to milestones and stop conditions;
- keeps public, deck, data-room and internal claim surfaces separate.
