# AI Data Protection Addendum — checklist advisory

> No es texto contractual ni asesoría legal. Úsalo para preparar la revisión con un abogado habilitado, el proveedor
> GenAI y el cliente. La ley, los términos del proveedor y la ruta técnica deben verificarse antes de firmar.

## Scope and definitions

- [ ] Jurisdiction, governing law, client entity, Efeonce entity and service scope identified.
- [ ] “Customer Data” includes prompts, briefs, inputs, images, video, audio, music, voices, faces/likeness,
      outputs, rejected outputs, metadata, embeddings, moderation data, logs, custom models and fine-tuning files where applicable.
- [ ] Provider, model, endpoint, plan, region and enabled features are named or controlled by an approved allowlist.
- [ ] Public, internal, confidential and restricted/biometric data classifications are defined.

## Provider commitments

- [ ] No use of Customer Data for general model training or improvement, unless client expressly opts in.
- [ ] No use of Customer Data to fine-tune shared/provider models without express instruction.
- [ ] Retention period and purpose are specified for prompts, inputs, outputs, caches, history, backups, abuse logs and metadata.
- [ ] Zero Retention is stated only for the exact eligible route and lists moderation, abuse, legal and security exceptions.
- [ ] Human access by support, security, moderation and engineering is addressed.
- [ ] Subprocessors, data transfers and data-residency commitments are reviewed.
- [ ] Tenant/workspace/project isolation, access controls, encryption and audit evidence are addressed.
- [ ] Deletion, return, offboarding and custom-model deletion are executable and evidenced.
- [ ] Provider change notification covers terms, model, endpoint, region, subprocessor and retention changes.
- [ ] Indemnity, security incident notice and liability limitations are reviewed separately from data-use commitments.

## Efeonce controls

- [ ] Efeonce uses agency-managed accounts and approved plans only.
- [ ] Personal/free consumer tools are excluded from confidential client data.
- [ ] Efeonce maintains a provider record and AI Data Protection Pack for Tier 2/3 work.
- [ ] Input rights, voice/likeness releases and client warranties are separate from provider data commitments.
- [ ] Efeonce can suspend, replace, regenerate or remove an asset if a provider or rights issue emerges.
- [ ] Incident, takedown, notification, evidence preservation and escalation owners are named.
- [ ] Portfolio, case study, feedback, provider improvement and model-training permissions are separate opt-ins.

## Client-facing claim review

- [ ] The clause does not say data is never processed by a provider.
- [ ] “No training” is not presented as “zero retention”, “no human access”, “no subprocessors” or “exclusive copyright”.
- [ ] Any Zero Retention, residency or no-human-access statement is backed by route-specific evidence.
- [ ] The client receives the scope, restrictions, exceptions and evidence pack appropriate to the tier.
- [ ] Legal/IP and Security/Privacy approve the final wording before signature.
