# Contract and Consent Checklist

Use with `legal-privacy-ip-operator`. This is a drafting checklist, not pre-approved legal language.

## Client AI rider / SOW

- [ ] Client has given written permission for AI-assisted/generative production.
- [ ] Approved tools, categories, or enterprise requirements are named.
- [ ] Client warrants rights and permissions for all supplied inputs.
- [ ] Agency may process inputs only to perform the scoped services.
- [ ] Output ownership/assignment is limited to rights actually held or transferable.
- [ ] Non-exclusive or non-copyrightable AI material is described honestly.
- [ ] Territory, term, channels, paid usage, derivatives, localization, and exclusivity are explicit.
- [ ] Provider terms and material restrictions can be passed through to the client.
- [ ] No absolute originality, exclusivity, non-infringement, or copyright guarantee is implied.
- [ ] Human review, approval, disclosure, and replacement process is defined.
- [ ] Indemnity follows the source of the material and has agreed exclusions/cap.
- [ ] Portfolio, case study, training, and model-improvement permissions are separate choices.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, return, and provenance archive terms are defined.

## Voice / likeness / avatar release

- [ ] Identity of the performer/person is explicit.
- [ ] Recording or image source is identified.
- [ ] Synthetic generation, cloning, transformation, and editing are expressly permitted.
- [ ] Approved words/scripts, languages, emotions, gestures, and contexts are defined.
- [ ] Client, campaign, product, territory, media, term, and paid advertising scope are defined.
- [ ] Reuse, sublicensing, training, model retention, and derivative voice/face models are addressed.
- [ ] Compensation, credit, approval, withdrawal, takedown, and post-termination handling are defined.
- [ ] Sensitive-data and cross-border processing disclosures are included where applicable.
- [ ] The person knows whether the result will be disclosed as synthetic.

## Music and sound checklist

- [ ] Composition/lyrics cleared.
- [ ] Master recording cleared.
- [ ] Performer and producer rights cleared.
- [ ] Samples and sound effects cleared.
- [ ] Synchronization, public performance, broadcast, digital, and paid-media rights cleared.
- [ ] AI-generated music plan tier permits commercial use.
- [ ] Output is not knowingly imitating or reproducing a protected recording or identifiable performer.

## Incident clause topics

- [ ] Notice channel and response time.
- [ ] Temporary takedown authority.
- [ ] Agency right to replace, edit, regenerate, re-license, or remove the asset.
- [ ] Cost allocation for client-provided inputs versus agency/provider output.
- [ ] Preservation of evidence without exposing client confidential information.
- [ ] Client and agency approval required before relaunch.
- [ ] Portfolio/publicity pause while the issue is unresolved.
