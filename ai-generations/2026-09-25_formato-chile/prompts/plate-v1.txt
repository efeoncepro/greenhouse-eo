Photorealistic editorial photograph inside a contemporary creative design studio at night. Vertical 4:5 frame, camera at eye level, 35mm lens, natural perspective, real photographic depth, subtle film grain. NOT an illustration, NOT a 3D render, NOT flat vector.

SCENE: a large matte wall painted very dark navy blue. Pinned to the wall with small black binder clips hangs a row of four printed poster proofs of the SAME advertising photograph, each in a different aspect ratio, left to right: a portrait 4:5 print, a square 1:1 print, a tall 9:16 print, and a wide 16:9 print. The advertising photograph on every print shows one woman in a bright orange coat standing on a rocky ridge, one arm raised pointing to the sky, against a clear deep blue sky. Each print is RECOMPOSED for its own shape: the woman always keeps normal human proportions; only the framing and the amount of sky and rock change.

THE HERO OBJECT: on the right side of the wall, an extraordinarily tall and extremely thin printed banner of the same photograph hangs from a ceiling rail that is above the top edge of the frame, and runs straight down to the polished concrete floor, where its last part curls gently onto the floor. The banner is about as thin as a hand is wide, many meters tall. On this thin banner the same woman in the orange coat appears SMALL near the bottom part of the banner, full body, with perfectly normal human proportions, NOT stretched, NOT squeezed, and the whole rest of the banner above her is deep blue sky fading to darker blue at the top. It must read clearly as a proof that was recomposed for an absurd format, not distorted.

A DESIGNER stands on the floor near the base of the thin banner, seen from behind in three-quarter view, wearing a plain dark charcoal hoodie with no print, head tilted back looking up along the banner, one hand holding a closed tablet at his side. Natural relaxed posture.

LIGHT: gallery-style ceiling track spotlights with neutral-warm white light create soft pools of light on the four prints and a narrow vertical wash of light along the thin banner. The upper part of the wall falls off into deep shadow. Polished dark grey concrete floor with soft reflections.
