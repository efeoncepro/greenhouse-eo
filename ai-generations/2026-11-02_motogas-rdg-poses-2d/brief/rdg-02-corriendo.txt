The reference image is the official mascot "El Rey del Gas": a flat 2D vector cartoon of a red LPG gas cylinder with a tilted yellow crown. Redraw THIS SAME CHARACTER in a new pose, as if the original illustrator drew another page of the same model sheet.

STYLE — non-negotiable, identical to the reference:
- Flat 2D vector illustration, cel style, 1930s rubber-hose cartoon mascot. NO gradients, NO shading, NO highlights, NO glow, NO texture, NO 3D, NO drop shadow on the floor.
- Exactly four colors and nothing else: pure red #FF0000, crown yellow #FFC214, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000. Every area is one solid color.

- Background: one flat uniform medium gray #808080 filling the whole canvas. No floor, no ground line, no shadow, no vignette.
- No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.

CHARACTER — keep every trait exactly:
- Body: a red gas cylinder, slightly tapered, about 2.3 body-widths tall including crown and shoes, with the black horizontal band near the bottom and the red handle/valve guard on top acting as its neck.

Center the full character in the frame with generous gray margin on all sides; nothing touches or is cut by the edges.

NEW POSE — "Running":
