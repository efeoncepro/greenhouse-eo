Images 1 and 2 are the exact design reference for the BRÍO can: a deep royal-blue aluminium can with a white sun symbol and the white vertical wordmark "BRÍO". BRÍO is a fictional lemon-lime soda. Reproduce that can design faithfully on every can; do not invent another logo.

A real, wide cinematic photograph (16:9) of a large evening brand activation for BRÍO in an open city plaza in Santiago de Chile at blue hour, the Andes a faint dark silhouette against a deep blue sky.

The activation is built the way real event productions are built. On the right half of the frame: two converted shipping containers painted royal blue, joined into an open bar with a bright yellow counter, their side walls swung open; on top of the containers, mounted on a black aluminium truss, a large illuminated sculpture of the white sun symbol about four metres wide, made of white LED tubes, glowing. Behind the bar, a lit fridge wall full of BRÍO cans. Promoters in plain yellow t-shirts with no print hand chilled BRÍO cans across the counter to a lively crowd of people of mixed ages; several people raise their phones to photograph the glowing sun, two friends clink their cans and laugh. Event lighting on stands, cable ramps on the ground, a light haze in the air so two narrow spotlight beams from the truss are visible.

Composition: the activation and the crowd sit in the right 60% of the frame. The left 40% is calm and dark: open deep-blue sky above and the dark, empty stone paving of the plaza below, with no people, no lights and no objects, so a headline can sit there. Camera slightly low, about 1.4 m, 35 mm lens, f/2.8, real depth of field, the nearest people softly out of focus at the right edge.

Realism: real event photography, natural film grain, painted steel with small scuffs on the containers, visible bolts on the truss, slight motion blur on moving hands, true night exposure with deep shadows.
