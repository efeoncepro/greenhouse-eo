BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: a steep stone-stepped street in the San Blas neighbourhood of Cusco, Peru, whitewashed walls with blue doors and Inca stone bases. walking up slowly at altitude on the first morning, a companion a few steps ahead pauses on a step to catch her breath, one hand resting flat on the whitewashed wall beside her shoulder, the street climbing behind her. The photographer climbs the same steps three steps behind her, phone held at chest height. Attention target: the next stone step just ahead of her feet. Photo type D — POV de recorrido.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, a terracotta pot of red geraniums on the edge of a step enters from lower-right corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: mid-morning, clear high-altitude sun with crisp dry air, deep blue sky; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

HERO SPACE (cover image, planned from the first moment): the website crops this photo to a wide panorama and lays a white headline over its lower half. Place the subject and its critical detail (faces, hands, contact, the landmark's recognisable shape) in the UPPER HALF of the frame and within the central 70 percent of the width, but never touching the top: the website also trims about the top eighth of the frame, so keep a margin of sky, wall or background above every head, raised hand and landmark top. Keep the lower half calm and continuous — ground, water, street, sand or the out-of-focus foreground — with no faces, hands, signs or busy high-contrast detail there, and no bright sky or white surface filling it.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
