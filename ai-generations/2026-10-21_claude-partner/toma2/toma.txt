A 5-second, extremely high-energy cinematic shot. The first frame is inside a murmuration of starlings at blue hour: huge birds flying past the lens, a tunnel of swirling birds, the terracotta sun at the center. The last frame is a wide, locked composition: deep navy sky, wind-sculpted dunes, the terracotta sun disc half sunk on the horizon, and the whole murmuration forming one giant curling wave over the sun.
Action:
0.0-2.2 s: the camera flies BACKWARD at great speed out of the flock, like a racing drone pulling back: dozens of starlings whip past the lens in every direction, wings beating hard, close birds streaking out of frame with heavy motion blur, the tunnel of birds rushing toward the camera and opening up.
2.2-3.0 s: the camera bursts out of the flock into the open sky and keeps pulling back, revealing the dunes, the horizon and the gigantic murmuration rolling upward.
3.0-3.4 s: the murmuration whips up into a towering wave and crashes over the sun; at the moment it breaks, the sun flares brightly and a gust of sand blasts across the dunes.
3.4-5.0 s: the camera settles to a complete stop on the final wide composition while the wave curls into its final shape and the birds keep streaming along its edges.
Photorealistic, powerful, fast, dramatic and elegant. Deep navy sky; the only warm colors are the sun and thin rims of light on the birds. No text, no logos, no people.
