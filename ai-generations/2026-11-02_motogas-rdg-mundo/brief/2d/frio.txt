STYLE — non-negotiable, identical to the reference:

- Only these colors: pure red #FF0000, yellow #FFC214, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000 (unless a different color is explicitly requested below). Every area is one solid color.

- Face exactly in the style of the reference: two big white eyes joined like a figure "8" with black oval pupils, one big open "D" smile with white teeth and a red tongue (or the expression requested). No nose, no ears, no eyebrows unless requested.

- The character is NOT the King: he does NOT wear a crown. Center the full character with generous gray margin; nothing touches the edges.
