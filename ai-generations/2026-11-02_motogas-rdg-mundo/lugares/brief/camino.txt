SCENE — THE ROYAL ROAD (images: 1 the royal road carpet, 2 the red tower castle, 3 the orange tower castle, 4 the kingdom diorama, 5 the palace).
The red velvet road with satin gold borders of image 1 starts wide at the bottom center of the frame and winds gently to the back, through a cheerful toy neighborhood of small cream houses with red roofs and round satin trees (style of image 4). On the LEFT of the road, in the middle ground, the RED tower castle of image 2; on the RIGHT, at the same distance and the same size, the ORANGE tower castle of image 3 — the two castles face each other across the road like two gatekeepers, each one large (about 40% of the frame height), each with its own small cream path joining the road. Far behind, on a gentle hill at the end of the road, the palace of image 5, small and softly out of focus. Little crown-topped gold street lamps along the road.
Reserves: the TOP 24% of the frame is calm warm sky with soft clouds and nothing else. The BOTTOM 14% is the wide near end of the road and the ground beside it, calm and slightly out of focus. The middle of the road between the two castles stays EMPTY (a character will stand there later).
