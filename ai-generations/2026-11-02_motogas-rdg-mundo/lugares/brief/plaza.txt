SCENE — THE ROYAL PLAZA (images: 1 the palace, 2 the clock tower, 3 the throne, 4 the kingdom diorama for the houses).
A sunny plaza paved with big rounded cobblestones in cream and soft terracotta. On the RIGHT, the tall clock tower of image 2, large and close, rising from the plaza into the upper right corner, its round clock face (only gold tick marks, red hands at ten o'clock) clearly visible. In the CENTER-LEFT middle ground, the royal throne of image 3 on a low round cream dais with a short red carpet, EMPTY, facing the camera at a slight three-quarter angle, big and proud. Behind them, at a distance and softly out of focus, the palace of image 1 with red velvet pennants, and a few little houses with red roofs and round satin trees.
Reserves: the TOP 24% of the frame is calm warm sky with soft clouds and nothing else (only the very top of the clock tower crown may enter the upper right corner). The BOTTOM 14% is calm cobblestone ground in soft shadow, slightly out of focus, with nothing on it. Leave a clear empty patch of plaza just to the right of the throne's dais (lower center) for a small character to stand later.
