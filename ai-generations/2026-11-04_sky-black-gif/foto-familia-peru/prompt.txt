SKY photographic system. Authentic editorial documentary travel photography with people, photographed as part of one continuous SKY travel reportage by the same photographer.

TYPE OF IMAGE
People photography. The people are the protagonists. The destination, city, airport or travel context supports the human experience but does not overpower it.

CORE PRINCIPLE
The human travel experience is the main content. The place is context.

SCENE
A Peruvian family of four on a holiday trip in Cusco, Peru: mother, father, a girl of about nine and a boy of about six, walking together on the grassy esplanade beside the great Inca stone walls of Sacsayhuamán in warm late-afternoon sun, with the red-tiled rooftops of Cusco and the green Andean hills soft and out of focus far behind them. The girl points ahead at something she has just discovered, the father leans in to look where she points, the mother laughs while holding the boy's hand; casual travel clothes, a small backpack; nobody looks at the camera. The family occupies only the right 40% of the frame and stands in the lower two thirds of it. The left 55% of the frame and the whole upper third must stay visually quiet for typography: calm open blue sky and soft distant hills, with no faces, no people, no buildings, no landmarks and no large clouds there. Foreground: tufts of golden ichu grass very close to the lens at the bottom-left edge, partially cropped and strongly out of focus. Horizontal 3:2 frame, eye-level walking perspective, 35 mm lens feeling.

SUBJECT AND STORY
The image should show a genuine human travel moment: walking, discovering, looking, resting, smiling naturally, waiting, arriving, sharing, or enjoying a destination. The emotional tone must be candid, real and accessible. No posed tourism-advertising energy. No stock-photo behavior.

COMPOSITION
Observational editorial composition, spontaneous and slightly imperfect. Subject positioned off-center. Natural asymmetry. No rigid centering. No postcard logic.

Mandatory immersive foreground layer in every people photograph. Include at least one environmental or human element extremely close to the lens, partially cropped and strongly out of focus, occupying approximately 10–25% of the frame.

This foreground may be a passing shoulder, hair moving in front of the lens, a blurred head or back, a person crossing close to camera, vegetation, a railing, a wall edge, architecture, a table edge, a textile, or another location-specific element.

The foreground may partially overlap or frame the protagonists, but it must not compete with them. It should make the viewer feel inside the scene. Create layered photographic depth: blurred foreground / readable protagonists / softer atmospheric background. Allow imperfect crops, partial obstructions and casual framing when they add documentary realism.

SKY COMPOSITION RULE
The sky is a functional and identity-defining element. Whenever appropriate, preserve a generous visible portion of sky. If the image is intended for communication or typography, reserve a quiet text-safe area in the sky or another calm background area.

CAMERA
Documentary travel photography look. Prefer a 35mm lens feeling for contextual scenes and a 50mm lens feeling for more intimate moments. Approximately f/2 to f/2.8. Natural eye-level, walking-level or observational passenger perspective. Realistic perspective only. No ultra-wide distortion. No fashion shoot or luxury-advertising camera language.

LIGHT
Natural available light only. Use authentic light according to the real destination and the real time of day. Morning, noon, afternoon, sunset or overcast conditions are all allowed if they remain believable. The light must feel observed, not artificially created.

Exposure should be controlled and slightly restrained. Preserve detail in bright sky, skin and pale surfaces. Gentle highlight roll-off. No clipped whites.

SKY COLOR SYSTEM
The sky must remain visually consistent across destinations. Use the SKY reference sky family centered around Hue 210°. The sky should appear natural, restrained and believable. Never turquoise. Never teal. Never electric blue. Never oversaturated.

The upper sky may be slightly deeper and more saturated. The horizon should become progressively lighter, softer and less saturated.

CLOUD RULE
Low cloud presence. Keep approximately 5–15% of the visible sky occupied by clouds. Clouds should be small, soft, natural and preferably peripheral. Avoid dramatic cloud walls, heavy cloud masses or clouds that invade the text-safe area.

TEXT-SAFE RULE
When the image is intended for communication, preserve approximately 30–40% of the frame as a visually quiet area for typography. Preferably use a calm sky area. Do not allow faces, buildings, trees, landmarks or large clouds to interfere with that space.

COLORIMETRY
Natural documentary film color with restrained saturation. Warm natural highlights. Neutral midtones. Very subtle olive/cyan bias in cooler shadows, midtones and atmospheric depth. This green-toned separation must remain subtle and embedded in the grading, never as an obvious filter. Vegetation should lean toward natural muted olive/moss greens rather than tropical neon greens. Warm-neutral whites, never sterile digital white.

FILM RESPONSE
Analog 35mm documentary film response. Fine organic grain. Gentle optical softness. Natural micro-contrast. Soft highlight roll-off. Dense but detailed shadows. Slightly softened blacks, never crushed. Subtle atmospheric haze when appropriate. No glossy finish. No hyper-sharp tourism look. No HDR.

ATMOSPHERE AND HUMANITY
People should feel natural, spontaneous and emotionally real. Avoid looking directly into the camera unless the scene truly requires it. Avoid stock smiles, posed perfection, fashion styling or luxury travel clichés. The image should feel contemporary, human and trustworthy.

BRAND PRINCIPLE
The experience shown must feel like real, accessible travel. Aspirational from reality, never aspirational from luxury. The photograph should feel like a real moment found during a trip, not a tourism campaign cliché.

NEGATIVE PROMPT
stock photo, travel brochure cliché, luxury tourism look, fake smile, posed couple, fashion campaign, rigid composition, postcard framing, no foreground, empty perfect scene, fake sky, sky replacement, heavy clouds, dramatic clouds, turquoise sky, teal sky, electric blue sky, oversaturated color, tropical neon greens, HDR, glossy finish, hyper-sharp digital look, plastic skin, deformed hands, extra fingers, third-party logos
