BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: Plaza de Armas of Cusco, Peru. the plaza nearly empty in the first hour of the morning, with the stone facade of the Cathedral and its two towers complete at the back and the hills behind; a woman traveler in warm clothes crosses the plaza diagonally holding a cup of hot drink; a few small distant people sweep, cross or open a door. It must read as a city just waking up, not an agency postcard. Photo type A — Destino contextual.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of the Plaza de Armas of Cusco with its Cathedral facade, towers, fountain and surrounding colonial arcades; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, the edge of a plaza bench enters from lower-left corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: first hour of the morning, cold air, clear dry-season sky, low luminous light with long but open shadows, highlights of stone and sky retained; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

HERO SPACE (cover image, planned from the first moment): the website crops this photo to a wide panorama and lays a white headline over its lower half. Place the subject and its critical detail (faces, hands, contact, the landmark's recognisable shape) in the UPPER HALF of the frame and within the central 70 percent of the width, but never touching the top: the website also trims about the top eighth of the frame, so keep a margin of sky, wall or background above every head, raised hand and landmark top. Keep the lower half calm and continuous — ground, water, street, sand or the out-of-focus foreground — with no faces, hands, signs or busy high-contrast detail there, and no bright sky or white surface filling it.

COMPOSITION, MEASURED: the Cathedral facade with both towers complete sits in the upper half, between 14% and 48% of the frame height and between 25% and 75% of the width, never touching the top edge (sky above the crosses). The woman crossing the plaza is small, at 35–48% of the height. The lower half is the calm plaza paving and the out-of-focus bench edge, mid-dark. SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, contextual and immersive.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
