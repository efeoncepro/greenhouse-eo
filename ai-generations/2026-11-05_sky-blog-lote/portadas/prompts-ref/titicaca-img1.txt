BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: edge of a Uros floating island on Lake Titicaca near Puno, Peru. dry golden totora reeds underfoot at the island edge and the open deep-blue lake toward the horizon; at one side the corner of a totora reed house and a neighbouring islet in the distance; a woman traveler in a jacket and knit hat stands looking at the water, at medium distance. It must read as high Andean plateau: high clean sky, strong light, dry air. Photo type B — Viajero integrado.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of the Uros floating islands: totora reed floor, reed houses and reed boats on the lake; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, bundles of dry totora reed enters from bottom edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: mid-morning, clear sky, high sun and cold wind, abundant light with open shadows, sky and water highlights retained; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

HERO SPACE (cover image, planned from the first moment): the website crops this photo to a wide panorama and lays a white headline over its lower half. Place the subject and its critical detail (faces, hands, contact, the landmark's recognisable shape) in the UPPER HALF of the frame and within the central 70 percent of the width, but never touching the top: the website also trims about the top eighth of the frame, so keep a margin of sky, wall or background above every head, raised hand and landmark top. Keep the lower half calm and continuous — ground, water, street, sand or the out-of-focus foreground — with no faces, hands, signs or busy high-contrast detail there, and no bright sky or white surface filling it.

COMPOSITION, MEASURED: the horizon line of the lake sits at about 45% of the frame height; the reed house corner, the neighbouring islet and the woman (small, head to feet between 22% and 48% of the height, centred between 40% and 60% of the width) all sit in the upper half. The lower half is the calm golden totora floor and the out-of-focus reeds, mid-tone, no faces or hands. SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, contextual and immersive.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
