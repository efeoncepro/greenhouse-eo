BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: comedor de una casa chilena en Nochebuena. una mesa larga de casa chilena puesta para la Nochebuena: el pavo dorado al centro, fuentes de papa mayo, palta reina y arroz árabe, un pan de pascua sobre una tabla y un jarro de cola de mono con vasos. Al fondo, una ventana abierta con cortina liviana movida por el aire y luz de tarde de verano entrando. Una persona deja la última fuente sobre la mesa. Se tiene que leer como hogar, no como restaurante ni catálogo. Personas: una persona de cuerpo parcial (manos, brazos y torso) dejando una fuente sobre la mesa, de espaldas o de perfil; nadie mira al lente; ningún rostro identificable. Photo type A — Destino contextual.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el borde de un vaso de cola de mono con gotas de condensación, enters from lower-right corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: tarde del 24 de diciembre, antes de la cena, con luz natural cálida y abundante que entra por la ventana (el «calor de diciembre por la ventana» del relato), sombras abiertas, sin quemar la ventana; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

HERO SPACE (cover image, planned from the first moment): the website crops this photo to a wide panorama and lays a white headline over its lower half. Place the subject and its critical detail (faces, hands, contact, the landmark's recognisable shape) in the UPPER HALF of the frame and within the central 70 percent of the width, but never touching the top: the website also trims about the top eighth of the frame, so keep a margin of sky, wall or background above every head, raised hand and landmark top. Keep the lower half calm and continuous — ground, water, street, sand or the out-of-focus foreground — with no faces, hands, signs or busy high-contrast detail there, and no bright sky or white surface filling it.

COMPOSITION, MEASURED: the subject and its critical detail (landmark, faces, hands, contact) sit only in the upper half, between 14% and 48% of the frame height and between 22% and 78% of the width, never touching the top edge; people are small enough to fit whole inside that band. The lower half stays calm and mid-toned (ground, water, street, table or the out-of-focus near element), with no faces, hands, signs or bright highlights. SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, contextual and immersive.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
