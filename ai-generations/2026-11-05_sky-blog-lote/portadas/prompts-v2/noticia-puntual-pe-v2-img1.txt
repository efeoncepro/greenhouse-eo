BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: wide, quiet departure lounge of a generic airport (must not imitate the Jorge Chávez terminal architecture). a traveler stands by the large window with a backpack on his shoulder and a boarding pass in his hand, looking outside; beyond the glass a pearl-grey sky and fine drizzle droplets on the window. It reads as calm waiting before an early flight, not as a delay. Photo type B — Viajero integrado.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, the backrest of a waiting-area seat enters from lower-right corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: pre-dawn early morning, Lima garúa drizzle, even diffuse light, luminous exposure without inventing sun; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

HERO SPACE (cover image, planned from the first moment): the website crops this photo to a wide panorama and lays a white headline over its lower half. Place the subject and its critical detail (faces, hands, contact, the landmark's recognisable shape) in the UPPER HALF of the frame and within the central 70 percent of the width, but never touching the top: the website also trims about the top eighth of the frame, so keep a margin of sky, wall or background above every head, raised hand and landmark top. Keep the lower half calm and continuous — ground, water, street, sand or the out-of-focus foreground — with no faces, hands, signs or busy high-contrast detail there, and no bright sky or white surface filling it.

COMPOSITION, MEASURED: the man stands farther from the camera; his whole figure, head to feet, spans from 18% to 48% of the frame height, centred between 42% and 58% of the width. Beyond the glass there is ONLY the pale grey drizzle sky and a distant blurred skyline: no aircraft, no apron, no jet bridge, no vehicles. The lower half is calm floor, mid-tone. SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, contextual and immersive.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
text, legible signs, logos, watermark, replaced sky, exaggerated HDR, aerial or drone view, people posing or looking at the camera, faces in close foreground, identifiable real people, saturated postcard colour, studio light, dull grey exposure, burned highlights, crushed shadows, flat gaussian blur, overlaid blur strip, visible halos or masks, duplicated objects, decorative artificial bokeh, blurry subject, foreground that covers the focal point, airplane, aircraft cabin, airline uniform or crew, sun or blue sky, readable boarding pass text, flight display screens or clocks with readable time, aircraft through the window, airline uniforms, aircraft of any kind, airport apron, jet bridges
