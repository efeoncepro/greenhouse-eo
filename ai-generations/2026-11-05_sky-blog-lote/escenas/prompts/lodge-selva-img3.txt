BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: Cama con mosquitero en una cabaña de madera de un lodge en la selva, con una lámpara encendida al anochecer. el interior de una cabaña de madera con paredes de malla hacia el bosque; una cama con el mosquitero recogido, una lámpara de luz cálida sobre una mesa pequeña y, detrás de la malla, el bosque ya oscuro con el último azul del cielo. La sensación de refugio en medio del bosque es el contenido: demuestra cómo es de verdad la noche en un lodge. Personas: sin personas: la cabaña vacía, como la encuentra el viajero al entrar; la mochila del viajero apoyada en una silla da la señal de POV. Photo type G — Pausa sensorial.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of una cabaña de lodge de la zona como referencia de materiales; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el borde del mosquitero muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde izquierdo, sin tapar la cama ni la lámpara enters from left edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: anochecer, justo después de que se va la luz del día, luz cálida de la lámpara combinada con el último azul del cielo, sin dejar la escena negra ni quemar la lámpara; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 50 mm equivalent.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
text, legible signs, logos, watermark, replaced sky, exaggerated HDR, aerial or drone view, people posing or looking at the camera, faces in close foreground, identifiable real people, saturated postcard colour, studio light, dull grey exposure, burned highlights, crushed shadows, flat gaussian blur, overlaid blur strip, visible halos or masks, duplicated objects, decorative artificial bokeh, blurry subject, foreground that covers the focal point, airplane, aircraft cabin, airline uniform or crew, sin texto ni logos, sin nombres de lodges, sin aviones, cabinas ni uniformes SKY, sin cielo reemplazado, sin stock, sin lujo de resort ni decoración de catálogo, sin insectos o animales dentro de la cabaña, sin aparatos electrónicos en primer plano
