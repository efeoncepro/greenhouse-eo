BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: Viajera recibe la primera ola fría en la orilla de Pinamar, en la costa atlántica de Buenos Aires, con médanos al fondo. la orilla de Pinamar con una ola que rompe en espuma alrededor de las piernas de una viajera, que encoge los hombros por el frío; al fondo, la playa ancha y los médanos con vegetación. El gesto del frío es el contenido: tiene que leerse que es el Atlántico, no un mar tibio. Personas: la viajera de espaldas o de perfil, con el agua a la altura de las rodillas, sin pose ni mirada al lente; bañistas lejanos y pequeños; ningún rostro identificable. Photo type G — Pausa sensorial.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of la playa de Pinamar con médanos; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, espuma de la ola muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde inferior, sin tapar a la viajera enters from bottom edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: media tarde de verano, con viento, cielo despejado o con nubes altas; luz abundante, sombras abiertas y altas luces conservadas en la espuma; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 50 mm equivalent.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
