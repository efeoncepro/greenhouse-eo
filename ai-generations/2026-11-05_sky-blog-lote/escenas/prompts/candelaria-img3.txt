BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Manos con guantes sosteniendo un vaso de bebida caliente frente a un conjunto que baila de noche en una calle de Puno durante la Candelaria. en primer término, las manos del viajero con guantes de lana alrededor de un vaso del que sale vapor; detrás, desenfocado, un conjunto con trajes de luces que pasa por la calle bajo el alumbrado público, con destellos de bordados y de instrumentos de bronce. La relación entre el vapor del vaso y la fiesta en movimiento es el contenido: demuestra el frío de la noche y por qué vale la pena quedarse. Personas: fotografía el propio viajero con la cámara a la altura del pecho; solo sus manos en cuadro; al fondo, danzantes y espectadores desenfocados, ninguno identificable; nadie mira al lente. The photographer — el propio viajero con la cámara a la altura del pecho. Attention target: en primer término, las manos del viajero con guantes de lana alrededor de un vaso del que sale vapor. Photo type E — Mano, objeto o alimento.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of la parada de noche en Puno para trajes y alumbrado; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el vaso y los guantes nítidos en el tercio inferior; el borde de la manga de la casaca muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde inferior izquierdo enters from lower-left corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: noche, después de horas de parada (el momento del relato), cielo despejado y frío seco; luz cálida del alumbrado y de la propia calle, sin flash, con detalle en las manos y destellos controlados en el fondo; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 50 mm equivalent, close lived detail of hands and objects, intimate without isolating them from the place.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
