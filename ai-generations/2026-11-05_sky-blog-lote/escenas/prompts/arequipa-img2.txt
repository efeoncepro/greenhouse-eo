BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Calle interior del Monasterio de Santa Catalina, en Arequipa, con muros rojos y azul añil, recorrida a pie por una visitante. una calle angosta y empedrada del monasterio, con muros pintados de rojo intenso a un lado y azul añil al fondo, macetas con geranios y una puerta de madera; una acompañante camina unos pasos adelante, mirando una de las puertas. El color de los muros es el contenido: tiene que leerse como Santa Catalina y no como un pueblo colonial genérico. Personas: fotografía el viajero que recorre la calle; la acompañante, de espaldas o de perfil, mira una puerta; ningún rostro identificable; nadie mira al lente; sin religiosas representadas. The photographer — el viajero que recorre la calle. Attention target: la acompañante, de espaldas o de perfil, mira una puerta. Photo type D — POV de recorrido.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of una calle interior del Monasterio de Santa Catalina; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, una maceta con geranios o el borde de un muro rojo muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde inferior derecho, sin tapar la calle enters from lower-right corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: media tarde de la primera jornada en el relato, cielo despejado; luz natural abundante que entra por la calle, sombras abiertas, color saturado sin quemar el cielo; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, body-height POV.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
