BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Manos abriendo las dos puertas de un retablo ayacuchano con santos en el piso de arriba y una fiesta del pueblo en el de abajo. un retablo clásico de dos pisos visto de frente y de cerca, con las dos manos del viajero terminando de abrir sus puertas pintadas de flores; en el piso de arriba, una fila de santos pequeños; en el de abajo, músicos, mujeres cantando y animales. La lectura arriba–abajo es el contenido: los dos pisos tienen que verse completos y nítidos. Personas: fotografía el propio viajero en primera persona; solo sus manos y el inicio de los antebrazos en cuadro; nadie más. The photographer — el propio viajero en primera persona. Attention target: un retablo clásico de dos pisos visto de frente y de cerca, con las dos manos del viajero terminando de abrir sus puertas pintadas de flores. Photo type E — Mano, objeto o alimento.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of un retablo ayacuchano tradicional de dos pisos (como referencia de estilo y estructura, no para copiarlo); keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el borde de la puerta izquierda del retablo, muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, sin tapar las figuras enters from left edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: media mañana, dentro de un taller, luz natural abundante desde una puerta o ventana lateral, sombras abiertas dentro de la caja, sin flash; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 50 mm equivalent, close lived detail of hands and objects, intimate without isolating them from the place.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
