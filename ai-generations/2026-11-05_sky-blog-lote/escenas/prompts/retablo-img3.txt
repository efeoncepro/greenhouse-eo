BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Manos de un artesano pintando figuras de pasta para un retablo ayacuchano, vistas sobre el hombro de una viajera en un taller de Ayacucho. una mesa de trabajo de taller con frascos de pintura de colores, pinceles finos y una fila de figuritas de pasta, algunas blancas y otras ya pintadas; las manos del artesano pintan una figura; la viajera, sentada al otro lado de la mesa, mira de cerca. Personas: fotografía un acompañante, de pie detrás de la viajera; la viajera, de espaldas o de perfil, mira las manos del artesano; el artesano aparece solo de pecho para abajo (manos, antebrazos y delantal), sin rostro; nadie mira al lente. The photographer — un acompañante, de pie detrás de la viajera. Attention target: la viajera, de espaldas o de perfil, mira las manos del artesano. Photo type F — Encuentro social.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of un taller de retablos de Ayacucho (mesa, figuras y pinturas); keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el hombro y el pelo de la viajera, muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde lateral derecho, sin tapar las manos del artesano enters from right edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: media mañana, dentro del taller, luz natural abundante que entra por una ventana o puerta, sombras abiertas, sin flash; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 35–50 mm equivalent.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
