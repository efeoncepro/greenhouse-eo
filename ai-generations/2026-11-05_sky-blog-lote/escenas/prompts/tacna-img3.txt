BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Plato hondo de picante a la tacneña con pan, sobre una mesa sencilla en un comedor de Tacna, con una cuchara en la mano del viajero. un plato hondo de picante a la tacneña, espeso y rojizo, con trozos de papa y carne, un pan al lado y la mano del viajero sosteniendo una cuchara; al fondo, desenfocado, un comedor sencillo con otras mesas. El plato es el contenido: tiene que verse como guiso casero del sur del Perú, no como plato de autor. Personas: fotografía el viajero sentado a la mesa; solo su mano en cuadro; al fondo, comensales pequeños y desenfocados; ningún rostro identificable; nadie mira al lente. The photographer — el viajero sentado a la mesa. Attention target: un plato hondo de picante a la tacneña, espeso y rojizo, con trozos de papa y carne, un pan al lado y la mano del viajero sosteniendo una cuchara. Photo type E — Mano, objeto o alimento.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el borde del vaso o de la mesa muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde inferior derecho, sin tapar el plato enters from lower-right corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: mediodía, la hora del almuerzo del relato, interior con luz natural abundante que entra por una puerta o ventana, sombras abiertas, sin flash; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 50 mm equivalent, close lived detail of hands and objects, intimate without isolating them from the place.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
text, legible signs, logos, watermark, replaced sky, exaggerated HDR, aerial or drone view, people posing or looking at the camera, faces in close foreground, identifiable real people, saturated postcard colour, studio light, dull grey exposure, burned highlights, crushed shadows, flat gaussian blur, overlaid blur strip, visible halos or masks, duplicated objects, decorative artificial bokeh, blurry subject, foreground that covers the focal point, airplane, aircraft cabin, airline uniform or crew, catalogue-coordinated outfits, presenting objects to the lens, vacant gaze, eyes diverging from the same target, milky irises, plastic skin, beauty filter, pore grid, aggressive micro-contrast, malformed hands, impossible grips, sin texto ni logos, sin cartas ni rótulos legibles de restaurantes reales, sin aviones, cabinas ni uniformes SKY, sin cielo reemplazado, sin stock, sin emplatado de revista ni vajilla de diseño, sin ingredientes que no lleve el plato
