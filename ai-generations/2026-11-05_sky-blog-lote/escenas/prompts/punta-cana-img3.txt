BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

SCENE: Viajero de pie en la orilla de Playa Macao, en Punta Cana, mirando el oleaje con espuma y viento. la playa abierta de Macao con olas que rompen y espuma blanca, la arena revuelta por el viento y vegetación baja detrás; un viajero de pie en la orilla, con la ropa movida por el viento, mira el mar. El contraste con el agua quieta de Juanillo y de la bahía de Saona es el argumento: tiene que leerse como otra playa, más salvaje. Personas: el viajero de espaldas o de perfil, sin pose ni mirada al lente; nadie dentro del agua; ningún rostro identificable. Photo type G — Pausa sensorial.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of Playa Macao; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, pasto costero o arena levantada muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde inferior, sin tapar al viajero enters from bottom edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: tarde, cielo con nubes altas y viento, luz abundante, sombras abiertas y altas luces conservadas en la espuma; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 50 mm equivalent.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
text, legible signs, logos, watermark, replaced sky, exaggerated HDR, aerial or drone view, people posing or looking at the camera, faces in close foreground, identifiable real people, saturated postcard colour, studio light, dull grey exposure, burned highlights, crushed shadows, flat gaussian blur, overlaid blur strip, visible halos or masks, duplicated objects, decorative artificial bokeh, blurry subject, foreground that covers the focal point, airplane, aircraft cabin, airline uniform or crew, sin texto ni logos, sin rótulos legibles de negocios reales, sin aviones, cabinas ni uniformes SKY, sin cielo reemplazado, sin stock, sin surfistas en primer plano, sin bañistas en el agua, sin cuatrimotos ni excursiones con marca
