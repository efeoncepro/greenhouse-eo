Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER D — observation: the place or narrated detail leads the photograph, observed from where a traveler can actually stand. People, if present, act spontaneously (walking, looking, talking, taking a photo); nobody poses or looks at the lens. Not everyone has to be turned away or out of focus.

Personas: fotografía el viajero desde el sendero; nadie dentro del canal ni tocándolo; si aparece otra persona, lejana y pequeña en el sendero, caminando; ningún rostro identificable. Photo type D — POV de recorrido.
