BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Mujer mayor en silla de ruedas empujada por una persona del aeropuerto por un pasillo de la terminal, con su acompañante caminando al lado y su bastón sobre las piernas. pasillo amplio de terminal. La mujer de la IMG 1, con el mismo chaleco gris, va sentada en una silla de ruedas estándar de aeropuerto, de espaldas o de tres cuartos de espalda, con el bastón de madera cruzado sobre las piernas. Una persona del aeropuerto, con ropa lisa y neutra y recortada desde los hombros hacia abajo, empuja la silla. A su lado camina el acompañante, del que se ven el brazo y el costado, con un bolso de mano colgado del hombro y la mano apoyada en el respaldo. Pasajeros ambientales con maletas, fuera de foco. Personas: fotografía el acompañante, que mira hacia adelante, al pasillo; la mujer mayor mira el camino; la persona del aeropuerto mira hacia el control; nadie mira al lente; sin rostros reconocibles. The photographer — el acompañante. Attention target: hacia adelante, al pasillo. Photo type C — Acción espontánea.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, la correa del bolso del acompañante muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por un borde lateral, sin tapar la silla enters from bottom edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: de día, interior de terminal, luz abundante por un ventanal lateral, sombras abiertas, altas luces conservadas; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
text, legible signs, logos, watermark, replaced sky, exaggerated HDR, aerial or drone view, people posing or looking at the camera, faces in close foreground, identifiable real people, saturated postcard colour, studio light, dull grey exposure, burned highlights, crushed shadows, flat gaussian blur, overlaid blur strip, visible halos or masks, duplicated objects, decorative artificial bokeh, blurry subject, foreground that covers the focal point, airplane, aircraft cabin, airline uniform or crew, catalogue-coordinated outfits, presenting objects to the lens, vacant gaze, eyes diverging from the same target, milky irises, plastic skin, beauty filter, pore grid, aggressive micro-contrast, malformed hands, impossible grips, sin texto ni logos, sin uniformes, logos ni colores corporativos de SKY o de otras aerolíneas, sin credenciales legibles, sin señalética ni pantallas de vuelos, sin aviones ni cabinas, sin cielo reemplazado, sin stock, sin sillas con marca visible, sin escena dramática ni de hospital
