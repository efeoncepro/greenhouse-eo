BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Agua verde y transparente de la bahía de Paraty vista desde la borda de un barco de madera, con una isla con selva al fondo. la vista desde la borda de un barco de madera hacia el agua verde y transparente, con el fondo visible y algún pez; al fondo, una isla cubierta de selva hasta la arena. El cambio de color del agua es el contenido. Personas: fotografía el viajero asomado a la borda, mirando hacia abajo; otro pasajero, de perfil o de espaldas, mira el agua o fotografía la isla; el conductor del barco no aparece; nadie mira al lente ni posa. The photographer — el viajero asomado a la borda. Attention target: hacia abajo. Photo type D — POV de recorrido.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, el borde de madera de la borda y un tramo de cuerda, muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, en la esquina inferior, sin tapar el agua enters from bottom edge, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: media mañana, cielo despejado y mar calmo (el día de bahía del relato), sombras abiertas, altas luces conservadas en los reflejos; verdes y azules naturales, sin saturación de postal; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, body-height POV.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.

NEGATIVE
text, legible signs, logos, watermark, replaced sky, exaggerated HDR, aerial or drone view, people posing or looking at the camera, faces in close foreground, identifiable real people, saturated postcard colour, studio light, dull grey exposure, burned highlights, crushed shadows, flat gaussian blur, overlaid blur strip, visible halos or masks, duplicated objects, decorative artificial bokeh, blurry subject, foreground that covers the focal point, airplane, aircraft cabin, airline uniform or crew, catalogue-coordinated outfits, presenting objects to the lens, vacant gaze, eyes diverging from the same target, milky irises, plastic skin, beauty filter, pore grid, aggressive micro-contrast, malformed hands, impossible grips, sin texto ni logos, sin rótulos legibles de negocios reales, sin aviones, cabinas ni uniformes SKY, sin cielo reemplazado, sin stock, sin nombre ni logo de barcos u operadores, sin parlantes a la vista, sin vista aérea ni de dron, sin agua de color irreal
