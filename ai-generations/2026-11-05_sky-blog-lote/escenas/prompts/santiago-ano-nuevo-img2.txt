BASE
Candid documentary travel photograph taken by a traveler who is a good photographer with a latest-generation phone, from the traveler's own point of view at an accessible position during the visit; not an advertisement, not a staged shoot. Luminous believable exposure, open shadows with real volume and detail, highlights retained, white balance coherent with the stated hour and weather, natural clean colour, moderate contrast, faithful skin tones, slight natural grain. Never change the hour, invent sun or replace the sky. Roughly 28–35 mm equivalent field of view, natural perspective, eye level unless the scene says otherwise, slightly asymmetric observational framing.

REGISTER E — immersive travel experience: the camera belongs to someone taking part in the trip. One or a few fictional adult companions are absorbed in one concrete action at an intermediate moment, with visible contact and a visible consequence. Each relevant person looks at a real declared target (the next step, the wave, the ball, the route, the person they talk to); head and both visible eyes agree with that target and its distance. Expression follows what happens. Discreet quality: well-made unbranded linen, cotton or technical clothing, individual and functional for the weather, never coordinated like a catalogue. No portrait, no presenting objects to the camera.

SCENE: Fuegos artificiales sobre la Torre Entel a medianoche vistos desde la multitud en la Alameda de Santiago. la vista del viajero desde el medio de la gente en la Alameda: la Torre Entel con fuegos artificiales abriéndose a su alrededor, y la multitud en primer y segundo plano con brazos levantados y celulares grabando. La torre tiene que ser reconocible; los fuegos, de colores, sin humo que la tape entera. Personas: fotografía el viajero desde la multitud, con la cámara a la altura de la cabeza, mirando hacia la torre; las personas delante miran hacia los fuegos o graban con el celular, de espaldas o de perfil; nadie mira al lente; ningún rostro identificable en primer plano. The photographer — el viajero desde la multitud, con la cámara a la altura de la cabeza. Attention target: hacia la torre. Photo type D — POV de recorrido.

REFERENCE ROLES: the attached real photograph (REF-01) is geographic evidence of la Torre Entel de noche desde la Alameda; keep its actual forms, scale and relationships, do not copy its people or composition. Style follows the written rules, not the reference.

THREE OPTICAL PLANES: very close to the lens, una mano que sostiene un celular en alto, muy cerca del lente y muy fuera de foco, por el borde inferior derecho, sin tapar la torre enters from lower-right corner, VERY strongly optically out of focus, soft organic silhouette and colour masses, natural size, occupying no more than about 12 percent of the frame; it never crosses the subject's face, hands, contact point or the landmark. Behind it, the subject and its key detail are sharp. Further back, the destination stays recognisable and loses fine detail gradually with distance, never a featureless portrait blur. Depth comes from real distance and focus: no gaussian strip, halo, pasted blur, mask or decorative bokeh circles.

LIGHT: medianoche del 31 de diciembre, cielo despejado de verano, la luz la dan los fuegos y la ciudad: altas luces de la pirotecnia conservadas, sombras abiertas en la gente, sin negro empastado; luminous exposure, open shadows with volume, highlights retained.

SKY BRANDLIFT CONSISTENCY (Daniela Ferreira, SKY Blog visual system, sheet 15): believable traveler POV at eye height; luminous natural light with open shadows and whites that keep detail; natural colour where ochres, blues and skin keep their true tones, moderate contrast, no filter, no orange-teal grade, no HDR; real optical depth: the near element very out of focus with soft optical bokeh, the subject sharp; a spontaneous everyday moment, no pose. LENS: 28–35 mm equivalent, body-height POV.

OUTPUT: native horizontal 16:9 master at the highest available resolution (3840×2160 when the engine allows it); no text, logo or border.
