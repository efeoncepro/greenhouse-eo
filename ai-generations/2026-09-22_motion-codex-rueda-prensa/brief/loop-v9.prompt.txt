THE SET (from image 2, locked): a small blue cloud-shaped vinyl character stands behind a dark lectern at a press conference, four foam microphones angled toward it, a glowing horizontal search bar across the lectern, black empty background. The camera is LOCKED OFF for the whole shot: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly. The character never changes size or position in frame; only its arms, its slight lean and its screen-face move.

THE PERFORMANCE — the character is an official spokesperson reading a routine statement about someone else. It is NOT celebrating, NOT happy, NOT triumphant: it is professional, detached and slightly bored, the way a press officer delivers an announcement they have given a hundred times. It NEVER looks at the camera — it addresses a press room that is somewhere off to the side, never us:
- 0.0–1.0s   At rest behind the lectern, facing slightly off-camera, arms down, screen-face showing the neutral prompt glyphs (pose of image 4).

- 6.0–13.0s  It holds still, facing slightly away, as if the statement is over and NO questions will be taken. Only the tiny blinking cursor in the bar and an almost imperceptible settle of its body show the shot is alive. Nobody asks anything.
- 13.0–15.0s It settles back into the exact rest pose of the first frame, unchanged and unbothered.
The gestures are small, contained and bureaucratic — readable in silhouette but never exuberant. Its body stays planted behind the lectern the whole time.

THE SEARCH BAR stays COMPLETELY EMPTY for all fifteen seconds: its small text cursor blinks slowly and no words, letters or query ever appear inside it. This emptiness is the point of the piece.
