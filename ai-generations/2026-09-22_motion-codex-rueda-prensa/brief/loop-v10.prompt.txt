A 15-second seamless loop for a vertical social ad. THIS IS A SILENT FILM WITH A MUSICAL SCORE: there is no dialogue track in this piece, nobody talks at any point, and the soundtrack consists only of instrumental music and physical sound effects. Treat it exactly like a 1930s animated short: the characters never speak, the orchestra and the foley carry everything.

Image 1 is the EXACT target look of the finished frame — its typography, its text hierarchy, its selection box and its cursors are the reference to reproduce faithfully. Image 2 is the clean set. Images 3 and 4 are the same blue cloud character in the exact poses it must hit.

THE SET (from image 2, locked): a small blue cloud-shaped vinyl character stands behind a dark wooden lectern, with several foam-topped stands angled toward it on slim chrome arms, and a glowing horizontal bar running across the lectern top. Black empty background. The camera is LOCKED OFF for the whole shot: no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly. The character never changes size or position in frame; only its arms, its slight lean and its screen-face move.

THE PERFORMANCE — the character is a mute mechanical figure going through a routine it has performed many times. It is NOT celebrating, NOT happy, NOT triumphant: it is detached, procedural and faintly bored. It NEVER looks at the camera — it faces slightly off to the side, toward a room we cannot see:
- 0.0–1.0s   At rest behind the lectern, arms down, screen-face showing the neutral prompt glyphs (pose of image 4).
- 1.0–3.5s   It leans forward and raises ONE short arm in a small, flat, matter-of-fact gesture, like someone ticking off an item on a list — never the arm of someone cheering (pose of image 3, restrained). Its screen-face glyphs flicker.
- 3.5–6.0s   It makes a second brief gesture — a short dismissive wave, as if closing the matter — and its arms come back down. Its screen-face stays NEUTRAL throughout: the prompt glyphs never turn into smiling or arched happy eyes at any point.
- 6.0–13.0s  It holds still, facing slightly away, as if it is finished and will take nothing further. Only the tiny blinking cursor in the glowing bar and an almost imperceptible settle of its body show the shot is alive.
- 13.0–15.0s It settles back into the exact rest pose of the first frame, unchanged and unbothered.
The gestures are small, contained and bureaucratic — readable in silhouette but never exuberant. Its body stays planted behind the lectern the whole time.

THE GLOWING BAR stays COMPLETELY EMPTY for all fifteen seconds: its small text cursor blinks slowly and no words, letters or query ever appear inside it. This emptiness is the point of the piece.

THE GRAPHIC LAYER — reproduce the typography, weights, sizes, colours and positions exactly as in image 1. The shape of this sequence matters more than any single timing: the lines arrive at a steady pace, then the FULLY COMPOSED frame is HELD FOR A LONG TIME so it can be read as a whole, and only then does it fade out SLOWLY. Once a line appears it never moves or leaves until the final fade.
1. 1.0s   The thin white line "Hoy alguien habló en nombre de tu empresa." fades in at the top.
2. 2.0s   The huge bold white headline "No fuiste tú." SLAMS in hard — a fast punch-in scale from 104% to 100% with no bounce. It is by far the largest, heaviest text in the frame.
3. 3.0s   A small teal arrow cursor labelled "IA" glides in from the right and a thin dashed selection rectangle with eight small square handles draws itself around "No fuiste tú.", then stays.
4. 4.0s   The line "Ni te preguntó nada." fades in below the headline.
5. 5.0s   The light blue line "Descubre qué dice de tu marca." fades in.
6. 6.0s   A lime-green rounded outline button reading "Pide el diagnóstico" fades in, with the small line "Diagnóstico SEO + AEO" under it.
7. 7.0s   The white arrow cursor glides to that button and clicks: the button's lime outline pulses once, brightly.
8. 7.5–13.0s  🔴 THE LONG HOLD. The complete composition — headline, all three lines, the selection rectangle, both cursors and the button — stays FULLY VISIBLE and PERFECTLY STILL for five and a half seconds. Nothing appears, nothing moves, nothing changes. This is the reading window and it is the most important part of the sequence: do not shorten it, do not animate anything during it.
9. 13.0–14.3s  A SLOW, GENTLE fade out: over more than a full second every element dissolves together and gradually — never a cut, never a fast wipe.
10. 14.3–15.0s The frame is COMPLETELY CLEAN and identical to the very first frame: only the character, the stands and the empty glowing bar. Do NOT leave any text on screen at the end.

TEXT SIZE AND LAYOUT — match image 1 exactly, do not shrink it:
- The whole text block occupies the upper 45% of the frame and is LEFT-ALIGNED, starting at 7% in from the left edge and 5% down from the top.
- The headline "No fuiste tú." is HUGE: its line spans about 62% of the frame width. It is by far the largest element in the picture — several times taller than any other line. Do NOT render it small or centred.
- The thin line above it is roughly one fifth of the headline's height; the lines below it about one fifth as well.
- The selection rectangle hugs the headline tightly with its eight handles on the corners and edge midpoints, and the teal "IA" label sits just outside its lower-right corner.
- The lime button is about 40% of the frame width, with its descriptor line directly beneath it.
Text is crisp, perfectly legible, correctly spelled Spanish, never warped and never handwritten.

SOUNDTRACK — two layers and nothing else. There is NO dialogue track: no voice, no speech, no gibberish, no babbling, no singing, no chanting, no crowd, no whispering. Not one syllable in any language is uttered at any point by anyone.

LAYER 1 — FOLEY, synced frame-by-frame to what MOVES. Every movement in the picture gets its own physical sound, and the character's movements are the LOUDEST and most present sounds in the mix:
   - a soft wooden thump at 0.0s as the shot opens;
   - a light airy whoosh at ~1.0s as the character leans forward;
   - a short bright xylophone or woodblock note at ~1.5s, landing exactly on the beat when its arm goes up;
   - a small descending swipe at ~3.5s as its arms come back down;
   - a soft mechanical settle at ~13.0s as it returns to rest.
   Quieter, for the interface: a delicate high tick each time a line of text appears (five of them, at 1.0s, 2.0s, 4.0s, 5.0s and 6.0s), a light pencil-like scratch as the selection rectangle draws itself at 3.0s, and a crisp mouse click at 7.0s when the cursor presses the button.
   If something moves on screen and you hear nothing, it is wrong. During the long hold (7.5–13.0s) there is NO foley at all — only the music.
   All of these are crisp, short and light. No comedy honks, no slide whistles, no boings, no slapstick.

LAYER 2 — MUSIC: a short, pompous institutional newsreel fanfare, stately brass and strings in the style of a mid-century official bulletin, solemn and self-important, as if something of great consequence were happening. The comedy comes from that solemnity being completely out of proportion to what actually happens, which is nothing. Restrained and dignified, NOT wacky, NOT a cartoon chase, NOT celebratory, NO marching band, NO bagpipes, NO singing, NO vocals of any kind. It must loop perfectly: the final bar leads straight back into the first with no gap and no fade. No ending sting: the loop is continuous.

Photographic, real physical object on a real set: subtle sensor grain, true depth of field with the foreground edge soft, real studio falloff into darkness. Not a cartoon, not a 3D render.
